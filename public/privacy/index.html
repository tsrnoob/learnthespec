Hosting and delivery
This site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. Server logs record IP address, time of request, page requested, referrer and browser string, for security and to keep the service running. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in a functioning, attack-resistant site. Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States. Personal data may therefore be transferred outside the EU; that transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and by Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46 (2)(c) GDPR.
Traffic measurement
We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which articles get read. It runs on every page without asking you first, because it stores nothing on your device: Cloudflare states that it uses no client-side state — no cookies and no localStorage — and that it does not fingerprint visitors by IP address or browser string. Visits are counted from the referring site rather than from anything tied to you, so § 25 TDDDG is not engaged and there is nothing to consent to. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in knowing which articles get read. You can object at any time under Art. 21; see Your rights below.
Consent management
Before any advertising or analytics loads, Usercentrics (Usercentrics GmbH, Germany) asks you to choose. It is a Google-certified consent platform and supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework v2.3. Nothing non-essential runs until you agree. To remember your answer, Usercentrics stores it on your device; that storage is strictly necessary for the consent function itself and is exempt under § 25 (2) TDDDG. Usercentrics is based in the EU, so no transfer to a third country is involved. Legal basis: § 25 (1) TDDDG together with Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the Privacy choices link in the footer of every page.
Advertising
With your consent we display advertising through Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited). Google may set cookies and process your IP address, approximate location, device and browsing behaviour to select ads and measure them. Without consent you see only non-personalised or limited ads. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR.
Analytics
With your consent we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) to count visits and see which pages get read. Nothing is fetched from Google and no cookie is set until you agree. For visitors in the EU, Switzerland and the UK, Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses: the address is used to derive a coarse location and then discarded immediately. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR. Withdraw consent at any time and collection stops. Data may be processed by Google LLC in the United States; that transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and by Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46 (2)(c) GDPR.
Newsletter
The newsletter runs on Buttondown. When you sign up we store your email address and send a confirmation link — nothing is stored as an active subscription until you click it (double opt-in). The confirmation is logged as proof of consent. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR. Your IP address is sent to Buttondown together with the signup so that its spam filter can score the request. Buttondown is based in the United States; that transfer is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46 (2)(c) GDPR.
Contact
If you write to us by email or through the contact form, we keep your message and address only as long as needed to answer it and to meet any retention duty. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) or (f) GDPR.
Your rights
You can ask for access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20), and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Consent can be withdrawn at any time with future effect. You may also complain to a supervisory authority — for us that is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Ansbach.
Keeping this page true

Every processor named above has to actually be in use, and every processor in use has to be named here. Adding an ad network, an analytics tool or a comment system means editing this page the same day.
