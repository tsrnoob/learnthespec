About Learnthespec

AI models, prices and hardware, compared from the source
What this site does
Vendors publish prices, limits and benchmark scores in different units, on different pages, and change them without notice. Learnthespec puts them side by side on one page, so you can see which AI model fits a job and what it will cost, without reading ten pricing pages yourself.
How articles are made
Every figure links to the page it came from: vendor documentation, price lists, model cards and public leaderboards. Each article states how many sources it was compiled from and the date they were checked. Where sources disagree, or where a number is the vendor's own claim, the article says so. We do not run our own benchmarks, and we say that on every page that ranks models.
Editions and updates
Prices and rankings change monthly, so comparisons are published as dated editions. When a newer edition exists, older ones say so at the top and link to it. Corrections are made in place, with a dated note at the bottom of the article.
Who runs it
Learnthespec is an independent site run by William Wu in Garching bei München, Germany. It is not affiliated with any AI vendor and is not paid to rank anyone. The site is funded by advertising, which never influences what an article says.
