Voice AI Prices, Compared, September 2026

Six vendors sell voice AI and no two bill it the same way. What each charges per minute of call, per million audio tokens, per hour of recording and per million characters.
Tsrnoob 20 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 8 SOURCES CHECKED 20 SEP 2026

Six vendors, four billing units

Voice AI covers three jobs: turning speech into text, turning text into speech, and holding a live conversation. The six vendors here sell all three between them.

Audio token — the unit Google and OpenAI bill audio in, instead of time. Both print a time rate beside it. Google charges $3.00 per 1M audio tokens of speech coming in, or $0.005 a minute.12

Four units are in use across the six. Which one a vendor picks decides what you can compare it against.

Per minute of call

Five of the six sell a finished voice agent priced by the length of the call.6 One does not.

Bundled voice agent — one product that listens, thinks and speaks, billed as a single per-minute rate. Deepgram's Standard tier charges $0.075 a minute for all three steps together.4

The spread across the five bundled prices is more than three times. Google's row is the odd one, because it never quotes a price for a minute of conversation at all.

Quality, on one index

Artificial Analysis scores these models on one composite index. It is the only figure on this page that ranks the models rather than their prices.

Speech to Speech Quality Index — a weighted average of four results: speech reasoning, agentic performance, arena preference and task success rate. A model missing any of the four gets no score. The top score today is 82.6.7

That top score belongs to a variant, not to the model that shares its name. Google puts it on Extended Thinking, and the plain model scores more than six points lower.8

Transcription, per hour

Transcription is the cheapest of the three jobs and the one with the most sellers. Four of the six vendors compete on it directly.

Streaming transcription — text returned while the speaker is still talking, rather than after a finished file is uploaded. AssemblyAI charges $0.45 an hour for it against $0.21 for the same model on a recording.5

The spread from cheapest to dearest is three times, and it tracks one thing: whether the text has to arrive live.

What the meter counts

A rate is half of a bill. The other half is what starts and stops the clock, and the six do not agree on that either.

One of them bills the connection rather than the audio.

Which one to pick

There is no single winner. The right vendor depends on which of the three jobs you are buying.

If youPickWhy
Want the cheapest conversationOpenAIgpt-live-1 is one flat rate for a minute of call, billed per second, and the lowest of the five bundled prices.
Want the highest-scoring voiceGoogleGemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking holds the top score on the only index that ranks these models against each other.
