Best AI for Images, September 2026

Ranked by people voting on images without knowing which model made them, then priced. The gap between the best model and the best downloadable one is far wider here than it is for text.
Tsrnoob 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 8 SOURCES CHECKED 4 SEP 2026

How these are ranked

Image quality has no benchmark score. It has votes.

Image Arena — Artificial Analysis shows people two images generated from the same prompt, without saying which model made either, and records which they prefer. Those votes feed an Elo rating, the system chess uses: a higher score means the model wins blind comparisons more often.1

It measures preference, not accuracy. A model can win on style and still get the number of fingers wrong.

The open-weight gap is enormous here

Every model in the top fifteen is closed. The best downloadable one sits at rank 28.23

That is a different picture from text, where the leading open-weight model is seven index points behind the overall leader and ranks near the top.8 In images the leader scores 1177 and the best open weights scores 1017 — 160 Elo points and 27 places apart.

OpenAI does not publish a per-image price

GPT Image 2 wins the arena. Its cost is billed per token, and OpenAI's pricing page lists no per-image figure, directing you instead to a calculator in the image generation guide.5

So the top-ranked model on this page is the one whose price you cannot read off a table. We are not printing an estimate in its place.

Per-token image billing — the image is charged as output tokens rather than as a unit. Cost then varies with resolution and quality setting, which is why a single number does not exist. Google publishes an equivalent anyway; OpenAI does not.

Quality and price do not track

Nano Banana 2 sits at rank 4 with 1121 Elo, twenty-two points behind Nano Banana Pro's stablemate at rank 9, and costs half as much.24

Within Google's own line-up the newer, cheaper model outranks the more expensive one. Paying more inside a single vendor's range does not reliably buy a better image.

Six companies, not two

The text-model comparison is dominated by four labs. Images are not. The top fifteen includes OpenAI, Microsoft AI, Reve, Google, Meta, Alibaba, ByteDance and xAI.2

Microsoft AI holds four of the top eleven places with its MAI-Image line, which does not appear in any text ranking on this site.
