4Which app it works in

Does the app around the AI matter?

IN ONE LINEYes. The AI doesn't touch your files itself. An app does that for it, and the same AI scores differently in different apps.
1 · The AI decidesIt reads the job and picks the next step.
2 · The app actsIt opens files, runs commands and shows the AI what happened.
3 · RepeatUntil the job passes or the AI gives up. The test scores both together.

Two apps ran 17 of the board's 23 results. Tap a block to see what each one is allowed to do.

!Side note

Why other sites show higher scores

The famous test stopped in February

SWE-bench Verified is the most quoted coding test. Its newest entry is from 26 Feb 2026, 234 days before this check. None of this year's flagship AIs is on it.

Companies grade their own homework

Anthropic ran the same test as the public board and published higher numbers for its own two AIs.

Other company figures (DeepSeek, Reflection, Anthropic's CursorBench and FrontierCode) have no public board to check them against, so this page does not rank them.
