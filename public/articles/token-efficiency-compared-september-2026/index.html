Token Efficiency, Compared, September 2026

Two models can charge the same per token and hand you bills that differ threefold. One of them thinks for longer, and thinking is billed.
Tsrnoob 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 11 SOURCES CHECKED 4 SEP 2026

The price per token is not the price

Every pricing page quotes dollars per million tokens. That is the rate, not the bill. The bill is the rate multiplied by how many tokens the model decides to spend.

Token efficiency — how many output tokens a model spends to finish the same work. Artificial Analysis publishes it as the total output tokens each model burned running its Intelligence Index, the identical suite of evaluations for every model.

Same tasks, same questions, same order. The only variable is how much the model wrote to get through them.

Verbosity is thinking, not padding

A verbose model is not writing you longer answers. It is writing to itself first.

Reasoning tokens — the working-out a model generates before it answers. OpenAI's documentation states they are not visible via the API, occupy space in the context window, and are billed as output tokens.1

You pay for text you never see. The effort setting on a reasoning model is the dial that decides how much of it there is, which is why the same model at a lower effort level costs less without getting shorter on screen.

The median is three different numbers

Artificial Analysis prints a median beside each model, and it is not one figure. It is the median of that model's own comparison group.

Proprietary flagships are measured against 79M. Large open-weight models against 140M. Smaller open-weight models against 170M.11

So "concise in comparison to the median" means different things on different pages. gpt-oss-120b is called very concise at 95M; Grok 4.6 sits exactly on its median at 79M, a lower number. Compare the tokens column, not the verdicts.

The open-weight models are the verbose ones

Every model above 130M tokens except one is open-weight. GLM-5.3 is the most verbose here, and Kimi K3 scores a point higher on fewer tokens.78

Cheap per token hides it. GLM-5.3 runs up a smaller bill than Kimi K3 despite spending more tokens, because the rate is lower — the two effects pull against each other, and only the bill shows which one won.

What this does not tell you

Token efficiency is measured on benchmark tasks, not on your work. A model that reasons less on maths problems may still reason heavily on your code.

The scores here are Intelligence Index v4.2, and they are not comparable to figures published under earlier versions of the index.
