Which models you reach

A free tier can be capped on volume or capped on model. Google and OpenAI chose opposite sides.

Context window — the text a model holds in one conversation, counted in tokens. One token runs about three to four English characters, so 27K tokens is roughly 12 pages of input by OpenAI's own reckoning.1

Google's free users reach every model its paid plans reach.3 Three vendors name no model on the free tier at all.

Run it yourself

The third route removes the vendor. Download the weights and run them on a machine you own.

Open weights — the trained model file is published under a licence, so it runs on your own hardware. No per-token fee, no usage ceiling, and nothing sent anywhere. Three of the four licences below are Apache 2.0.16

The hardware the cards ask for runs from one consumer card to two datacentre ones.
