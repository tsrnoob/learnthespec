A new scale and new judges

September's figures cannot be carried forward. The board now has a version string, a fixed anchor and a different set of voters.2

AA-Video-T2V v2.1 — Artificial Analysis's text-to-video benchmark with audio. Validated private evaluators compare two 10-second 1080p clips from the same prompt, drawn from 1,000 prompts, and Kling 3.0 1080p (Pro) is fixed at 1000 Elo. Public arena votes do not count.2

So the ranks are the part that translates. Several September leaders slid a long way.

First place is a range

The board prints a Range beside every rank, and it is wider than the Elo gaps suggest.1

Range — the ranks a model could hold once the uncertainty in every rating is counted. On the overall board Artificial Analysis gets it by simulating the full leaderboard; Wan 3.0's 1174 carries a 95% interval of 1161 to 1187.12
