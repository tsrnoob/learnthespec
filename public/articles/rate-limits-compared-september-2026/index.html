Rate Limits and Usage Caps, Compared, September 2026

The same company will tell you its API limit to the token, send you a live counter in every response, and then decline to say how many messages your subscription buys.

Limits exist on both sides of every AI vendor. Only one side is documented.

THIS IS NOT TWO COMPANIES. ANTHROPIC PUBLISHES BOTH COLUMNS, AND SO DO GOOGLE AND OPENAI.

What the consumer plans say

Nothing numeric. Anthropic states the position plainly.

“The number of messages you can send will vary based on your Claude plan.”2

ChatGPT marks features “Limited” and prices Pro at “5x or 20x more usage”.7 Google meters by compute and refreshes every five hours until a weekly cap.6 None of the three names the base.

One product on this page publishes a real number, and it is a coding tool: GitHub Copilot Free gives 2,000 completions and 50 chat requests a month.8

Anthropic will not give you a number, but it does list what costs you most. This is the most useful thing published about consumer limits anywhere.

CONVERSATION LENGTH IS THE ONE PEOPLE MISS. A LONG THREAD RESENDS ITSELF WITH EVERY MESSAGE, SO THE HUNDREDTH REPLY IN A CHAT COSTS FAR MORE THAN THE FIRST. STARTING A NEW CONVERSATION IS THE CHEAPEST FIX AVAILABLE.

You can watch your position in real time

Every API response carries headers naming the limit, what is left, and when it refills: anthropic-ratelimit-input-tokens-remaining, -limit and -reset, among others.1

Nothing equivalent exists on a subscription. The chat apps show a progress bar once you are already deep into the window.

Token bucket — the API's replenishment model. Capacity refills continuously rather than resetting at fixed intervals, so a burst can still trip a limit that looks unused on average. A 60-per-minute limit may be enforced as one per second.1

Caching moves the limit

The single largest lever on both sides, and only one side quantifies it.

On the API, cached input tokens do not count toward the input limit on most Claude models. Anthropic's own worked example: with a 2,000,000 ITPM limit and an 80% cache hit rate, you can process 10,000,000 input tokens a minute.1 Five times the throughput, same tier.

On a subscription the same mechanism exists without a figure: “Content in projects is cached and doesn't count against your limits when reused.”2

What happens when you hit it

Three different walls, three different exits.

Hitting the ceiling
WhereWhat happensHow you continueSrc
Claude planSession limit notificationWait for the reset, or enable usage credits and pay standard API rates[4]
Gemini planLimit reachedContinue on Flash-Lite, a smaller model[6]
API rate limitHTTP 429 with a retry-after headerWait the stated seconds, or raise your tier[1]
API spend capHTTP 429 with no retry-afterNothing until 00:00 UTC on the 1st, unless you request a higher limit[1]

THE LAST ROW IS THE TRAP. IT LOOKS LIKE A RATE LIMIT AND CARRIES THE SAME ERROR TYPE, BUT RETRYING NEVER SUCCEEDS — INCLUDING THE SDKS' AUTOMATIC RETRIES. THE GIVEAWAY IS THE ERROR CODE enforced_spend_limit_reached.[1]
