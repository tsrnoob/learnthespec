What Open Weights Actually Mean, September 2026

Open weights and open source are two different things, and almost every model called the second is really the first. Here is what each term requires, what licence each model ships under, how large the files are, and what you can do once one is on your own machine.
Tsrnoob 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 12 SOURCES CHECKED 8 NOV 2026

Two terms, one difference

A model has three parts: the finished parameters, the code that trained them, and information about the data. Which of those you get is the entire distinction.

"OPEN SOURCE AI" HERE MEANS THE OPEN SOURCE INITIATIVE'S DEFINITION, VERSION 1.0. IT IS THE ONLY FORMAL DEFINITION OF THE TERM.

What the definition requires

The Open Source Initiative published version 1.0 of its Open Source AI Definition, built on four freedoms: to use the system for any purpose without permission, to study how it works, to modify it for any purpose, and to share it with or without changes.1

To grant those, three things have to be released.

Data Information — “sufficiently detailed information about the data used to train the system so that a skilled person can build a substantially equivalent system”. Code — “the complete source code used to train and run the system”. Parameters — “the model parameters, such as weights or other configuration settings”, including training checkpoints. All three under OSI-approved terms.1

What open weights gives you

The third item only. You get the finished model file and nothing about how it was made.

Weights — the numbers the training process produced. They are the model. Download them and you can run it, but you cannot see what it learned from or repeat the process that made it.

The OSI names four consequences: you cannot replicate the development process, you cannot assess the data, weights alone may not satisfy transparency regulation, and outside contribution is limited to “superficial fine-tuning”.2

Its summary of the gap is direct.

Open weights “still stop short of delivering the level of transparency many researchers and regulators deem essential”.2

Downloadable does not mean unrestricted. The licence decides what you may do, and they are not the same across vendors.

Quantisation

Every size above is a compressed version. Compression is what makes local models possible at all.

Quantisation — storing the weights at lower precision to cut memory, “while trying to preserve as much accuracy as possible”. Weights are normally full precision (fp32); half precision (fp16) is common; some methods go to int8 or int4, and a few to one or two bits.8

Fewer bits means a smaller file and a less accurate model. That is the trade, and it is yours to set rather than the vendor's.

Total parameters are not running parameters

A headline parameter count no longer tells you what it costs to run.

Mixture of experts — only part of the model activates for any given token. DeepSeek V4 Pro has 1.6 trillion total parameters but activates 49 billion during inference.4 OpenAI's gpt-oss-120b has 117 billion total and 5.1 billion active.5

gpt-oss-120b is the clearest example of what that buys: OpenAI states it is designed to “fit into a single 80GB GPU” such as an H100 or MI300X.5 A 117-billion-parameter model on one card.

What changes when the file is yours

Four things, and none of them are available from an API at any price.

No per-token bill. You pay for hardware and electricity instead. Cost stops scaling with use.

No rate limit. No five-hour window, no weekly cap, no queue.

Nothing leaves your network. The relevant point for regulated work, private code and client data.

It cannot be withdrawn. A downloaded model stays exactly as it is. Hosted models are deprecated, repriced and retuned without notice.

What you give up

You supply the machine. The 671B file is 404 GB. That is not a laptop.

You supply the operations. Serving, updating and monitoring are now yours.

You cannot audit the training. Without data information or training code, the model's origins stay closed even when the weights are open.2

The top score is still closed. The best open-weight model scores 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index against 57 for the leader.10 The gap is seven points and it has been narrowing.
