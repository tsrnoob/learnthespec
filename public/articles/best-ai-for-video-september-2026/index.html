Best AI for Video, September 2026

Ranked by people voting on clips without knowing which model made them, then priced per second. A downloadable model sits fourth, most of the top fifteen comes from Chinese labs, and Sora is not in it at all.

How these are ranked

Same method as images. People watch two clips from the same prompt, without being told which model made either, and pick one.

Video Arena — Artificial Analysis collects those blind votes into an Elo rating. It runs two separate boards, one for models that output audio and one for models that do not. The table below is the with-audio board, because audio is now standard: Google's Veo includes it by default.14

A downloadable model is fourth

MiniMax H3 publishes its weights and scores 1227, eleven points behind the leader.2 A second open-weight model, MAGI-2 Preview, sits at rank 9.

Compare that with images, where the best downloadable model is rank 28 and 160 Elo behind.7 Video is the one medium where running it yourself costs you almost nothing in quality.

Chinese labs hold most of the board

Alibaba appears at ranks 1, 6 and 7. MiniMax at 3 and 4, ByteDance at 5, Sand.ai at 9, KlingAI at 10, Skywork at 12.2

Price does not follow rank

Veo 3.1 Standard costs $0.40 per second and ranks 14th. Veo 3.1 Lite costs $0.05 and ranks 15th, three Elo points behind it.24

Eight times the price for three points. Within Google's own range, the cheap model is nearly as good in blind tests.

Sora 2 Pro at 1080p is the most expensive option here at $0.70 a second, and does not place in the top fifteen.25

A minute of video is not cheap

At the top of the price table, sixty seconds costs $42.5 At the bottom it costs $3.4

Video is the most expensive thing on this site by an order of magnitude. One minute of Sora 2 Pro costs more than two months of a ChatGPT Plus subscription.

Which one to pick

The arena settles quality and the rate cards settle cost. They rarely point the same way.
