What AI Chatbots Can Actually Do, September 2026

The text box is the smallest part of it. Fourteen capabilities ship inside the four big assistants, and most people use three. Here is the full list, what each one is for, and where the limits sit.
Tsrnoob 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 11 SOURCES CHECKED 25 OCT 2026

Uploading files

The single most underused capability. You can hand the assistant a document and ask questions about its contents instead of pasting text.

File upload — the file's contents are read into the conversation and count against the context window, the amount of text the model holds at once. Claude accepts PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, HTML, ODT, RTF, EPUB, JSON and XLSX, plus JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP images.3

Limits are published and they are not small.

Reading images

Point a camera at something, or paste a screenshot, and ask about it. OpenAI calls this Vision and marks it “Limited” on free plans.1 Gemini works from a phone camera directly.5

Useful for error messages, forms in another language, handwriting, and anything faster to show than describe.

Making images, video and music

Google ships the widest set here: image, video and music generation all inside the app.5 OpenAI generates images in the chat, with a separate “Image generation with Thinking” mode on paid plans.1

Claude generates no images, video or music at all. It is the one clear capability gap in the group.

Searching the web

Every assistant here can look things up rather than answer from training data alone. That matters for anything that changed recently.

Training cutoff — the date after which the model learned nothing. Without web search, an assistant answering about last month is guessing from older material. Search is what makes a current answer possible.

Deep research

A separate, slower mode. It runs many searches, reads what it finds, and returns a cited report rather than a chat reply.

Deep research — a long-running task, typically minutes rather than seconds, that produces a sourced document. Every vendor meters it separately from ordinary chat: ChatGPT Plus lists roughly 10 full plus 15 lightweight runs a month, and Pro plans around 250.1

The count is the thing to check before paying. It is the most rationed feature on every plan.

Running code on your data

Upload a spreadsheet and the assistant can compute against it rather than guess at it. OpenAI and Anthropic both call this Data analysis.12

Code execution — the assistant writes a short program, runs it in a sandbox, and reports the result. Arithmetic done this way is calculated rather than predicted, which is why totals from a spreadsheet are reliable in a way mental-arithmetic answers are not.

Building something you can keep

Answers can come back as working objects instead of text: a calculator, a chart, a page, a document you edit in place.

Artifact (Anthropic), Canvas (Google), Interactive apps and Sites (OpenAI) — the same idea under four names. Output opens in its own panel beside the chat, keeps its own version history, and can be shared as a link.125

This is the capability that most changes what the tool is for. It stops being something that describes work and starts being something that hands work over.

Remembering you

By default each conversation starts blank. Memory changes that.

Memory — facts the assistant keeps between conversations, such as how you like answers formatted or what you are working on. OpenAI splits this into “Memory”, “Memory with past chats” and “Memory sources”, all marked “Limited” on free plans.1 Anthropic lets you export a memory and import it elsewhere.4

Custom personas

A saved set of instructions and files that the assistant loads every time. GPTs at OpenAI, Projects at Anthropic, Gems at Google.125

Worth setting up once for anything you ask repeatedly. Worth ignoring entirely otherwise.

Connecting your own accounts

The assistant can read your actual documents and messages, with permission, instead of working from what you paste.

Connector — an authorised link to an outside account. Anthropic ships pre-built ones for Google Workspace and GitHub, plus JIRA, Zapier and Intercom.24 MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is the open standard these are built on.8

This is also the capability with the largest privacy cost. Granting it means the assistant reads that account.

Working while you are away

OpenAI ships Scheduled tasks on paid consumer plans.1 Anthropic's recurring tasks are Team and Enterprise only.4 xAI describes Grok 4.6 as built for “long-running agents”.7

Agent — a mode where the assistant takes a goal and works through several steps on its own, rather than answering one message at a time.

What still does not work

Four things, all published rather than observed.

It can be wrong. Google states plainly that “Gemini Apps can make mistakes” and that answers should not replace professional advice.6

Signed out, most of it disappears. Google's own documentation says signed-out users get no chat history, no personalised responses and no file uploads.6

Free plans limit nearly every row above. OpenAI marks memory, vision, file uploads, data analysis and deep research all as “Limited” on Free.1

Anthropic now lists five of these rows on its own Free plan, with no limiting word beside any of them.11

Running out is normal. Usage resets on a rolling five-hour window at Anthropic and Google, with a weekly cap above it.910
