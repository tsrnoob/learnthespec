Scheduled AI Price Changes, November 2026

Only one vendor publishes what its API will cost on 1 January 2027, and every rate it prints there doubles. The rest date nothing, hedge it, or cancel it.
Tsrnoob 1 NOVEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 9 SOURCES CHECKED 1 NOV 2026

The rates that already have a date

Google prints the future price in the same table as the current one. Every published change is an increase, and every one is the same increase.

List price — the published per-token rate before any discount, commitment or marketplace markup. Google prints two of them for the same model: the one in force today, and the one that starts on a named date.1

Nothing here falls, and nothing moves by a fraction. The whole set doubles.

Introductory, with no end date

Four vendors say a rate is temporary. None of the four prints the day it stops.

Introductory price — a launch rate the vendor states is temporary. Gemini 4 Carbon's is 2.00 USD per 1M input tokens against a stated 4.00 afterwards, and the page gives no date for the change.2

Two of them do not publish the later rate either, so there is no figure to plan against at all.

The one increase that did not happen

A published date is not a promise in either direction. Anthropic announced a launch rate as introductory, scheduled the rise, and then printed a footnote saying it would not occur.3

The launch rate became the standard rate instead. It is the only cancelled increase on any page read here. A reader knows the rise was ever scheduled only because Anthropic left the note in.

Which one to pick

There is no single winner. The right choice depends on whether you need the price to hold.

Recommendation by use case
If youPickWhy
Need a budget for 2027GoogleIt is the only vendor here that prints the rate that starts on a named date, so the figure can go in a forecast.
Are budgeting on a Gemini Flash rateDouble itGemini 3.8 Flash, 3.6 Flash and both TTS models all double on 1 January 2027, and context storage doubles with them.
Want the cheapest rate in force todayClaude Haiku 5.5 or GPT-6 LunaBoth publish the same input and output rate, and neither carries a date that moves it.
