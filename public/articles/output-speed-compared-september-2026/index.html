Output Speed, Compared, September 2026

Speed is two numbers, and they measure different waits. At maximum reasoning effort the leading models take minutes before writing a single word, and the setting that fixes it costs almost nothing.
Tsrnoob 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 COMPILED FROM 9 SOURCES CHECKED 4 SEP 2026

Two numbers, two waits

Every comparison quotes tokens per second. That is the second half of the wait, not the first.

Time to first token — how long you stare at nothing before the reply starts. Output speed, in tokens per second, is how fast it writes once it has begun. A model can be excellent at one and poor at the other.1

Short question in a chat window? You feel the first number. Generating a long document? You feel the second.

The wait is measured in minutes

GPT-6 Astra at maximum effort takes 463.73 seconds before its first token. That is seven minutes and forty-four seconds of nothing.3

Claude Fable 5.1 at maximum effort takes 266.51 seconds, four and a half minutes.2 Claude Opus 5 takes 91.20 seconds.4

The median for comparable models is 3.86 seconds. The flagships at full effort are more than a hundred times that.

Fast and clever are opposites right now

Read the first table from the top and the pattern is hard to miss. The three quickest to respond score 16, 46 and 50. The three slowest score 54, 57 and 55.

gpt-oss-120b answers in 0.82 seconds and writes at 162.9 tokens per second — more than double the median on both counts. It scores 16 on intelligence, the lowest here.8

The delay is not overhead. It is the model thinking, and thinking is what the score measures.

The exception

Gemini 3.1 Pro writes at 116.0 tokens per second, the fastest of any flagship here, and still takes 30.82 seconds to start.9

Fast typist, slow thinker. It is the one model where the two numbers pull in opposite directions, and it explains why a model can feel sluggish and brisk in the same reply.

Best of both

Two open-weight models break the pattern. GLM-5.3-Flash answers in 1.64 seconds and scores 46.7 Kimi K3 answers in 4.72 seconds and scores 50.6

Both land within seven points of the leader while responding in seconds rather than minutes. Kimi pays for it in writing speed at 39.6 tokens per second, the slowest in the table.
