The rate is not the bill

Every pricing page quotes dollars per million tokens. That is the rate. The bill is the rate times the tokens the model decides to spend.

Token efficiency — the output tokens a model generates to get through the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ten evaluations run identically for every model on v4.3.2. Each model page prints the median of its own peer group beside that count.2

Thinking is billed

A verbose model is not writing you a longer answer. It is writing to itself first.

Reasoning tokens — the working-out a model generates before it answers. OpenAI's documentation states they are not visible via the API, occupy space in the context window, and are billed as output tokens. It recommends reserving at least 25,000 tokens for reasoning and output.1

You pay for text you never see. The token column above counts it.

Same tokens, very different bills

Output tokens do not decide the bill on their own. The rate does the rest.

Cost per task — the weighted average cost in USD of one Intelligence Index task, as Artificial Analysis publishes it across the ten v4.3.2 evaluations. The figure includes input, cache hit, cache write, reasoning and answer token prices.2

Three of these rows spend within ten million tokens of each other. One task costs sixty-two times what another costs.

The effort level picked these numbers

Artificial Analysis lists a reasoning model once per effort level. The token count and the cost belong to the level, not to the model.

Reasoning effort — how long a model may think before it answers, set per request. OpenAI documents the allowed values as none, minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh and max, and says support varies by model.1 Claude Opus 5.5 scores 51 at medium for 1.34 USD a task, and 58 at max for 5.98.3

Every row above names its level. A table that prints one score per model has chosen one silently.

What this does not tell you

Token efficiency is measured on benchmark tasks, not on your work. A model that reasons little on maths may reason heavily on your code.

The model pages no longer publish a total cost for the whole run. Two of them print “Not publicly available” where that figure sat.45

Scores and token counts here are Intelligence Index v4.3.2. They are not comparable with figures published under v4.2.2
