How the ranking is made

Image quality has no benchmark score. It has votes.

Image Arena — Artificial Analysis shows a screened evaluator two images made from the same prompt, naming neither model, and records which one is preferred.4 Every vote is fitted at once with a Bradley-Terry model and reported on an Elo scale. Elo is the chess rating, so a higher number means the model wins blind comparisons more often. The scale is fixed by holding FLUX.2 [dev] at 1000.1

OpenAI holds the first three places. Each row also carries an interval, and the intervals overlap.2

The board is now versioned

In September this board printed no version string.16 It prints one now.

AA-Image-T2I v2.1 — the current version of the text-to-image arena. It replaced v2.0 by moving the Elo calculation, the intervals and the Range column to a new statistical method. It also drops models with too few votes to rank.1

So the Elo figures here cannot be read against the ones this page carried in September.

Ranks four to six overlap

A rank on this board is a point estimate. The board publishes the span around each one as well.

Range — the span of ranks a model could plausibly hold. On the overall board it comes from simulating the whole leaderboard of 106 models, not from one model's interval alone.1

The spans at ranks four, five and six all include rank four. Three companies could hold one place.2
